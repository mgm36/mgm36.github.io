Associate Professor @ Pratt Institute | School of Information

Research Interests

My research addresses:

  1. Information technology education, trends, and applications in the information science field,
  2. The design and customization of information technology tools by end users, and
  3. Single-board computer and microcontroller applications in libraries, archives, and museums.
